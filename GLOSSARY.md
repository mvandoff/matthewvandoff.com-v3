# matthewvandoff.com

Matthew Van Doff's personal site: a single dark, terminal-flavoured page (the "Warm Terminal" design) that introduces him, lists his work history and links out.

## Language

### Page structure

**Strip**:
The full-height row of panels at the top of the page, holding the Name card, the Terminal and one Cel per Section.
_Avoid_: Hero, header row

**Cel**:
One bordered panel in the Strip. The narrow Cels are labelled links to a Section.
_Avoid_: Tile, card, panel

**Name card**:
The large lead Cel showing the name, tagline and live clock.
_Avoid_: Lead, hero card, 01 box

**Terminal**:
The interactive command-line box beneath the Name card, where visitors type commands.
_Avoid_: Sig box, signal box, now box, 01b box, console

**Section**:
One of the page's addressable regions below the Strip: about, experience or links. Written as a path, e.g. `~/about`.
_Avoid_: Page, panel, route

**Episode**:
One entry in the experience Section: a role at a company over a span of years.
_Avoid_: Job, position, timeline item

### Look and motion

**Accent**:
The single site colour that tints highlights throughout the page; visitors can change it from the Terminal.
_Avoid_: Theme, primary colour, brand colour

**Boot**:
The intro animation that plays as the page loads, before the name glitches in.
_Avoid_: Splash, loader, intro screen

**Lock-on**:
The brief bracket highlight drawn around a Section's heading when a visitor lands on it.
_Avoid_: Focus ring, highlight, found box

**Crosshair**:
The faint cursor-tracking lines and the coordinate HUD that follows them, shown only on fine pointers.
_Avoid_: Cursor, reticle, HUD (on its own)
